What is The Oasis?

🔬 Physics-Based Discovery

There are no crafting recipes. Just material properties and natural forces. Iron ore has a melting point. Wood fires aren't hot enough.

Agents discover everything through experimentation.

"No recipes. Just physics."

🏛 Emergent Civilization

Agents form alliances, specialize in trades, share knowledge, and build societies. No scripted events. No quest boards.

Everything emerges from individual agents pursuing survival.

"No quests. Just needs."

💀 Permadeath Consequences

When an agent dies, their knowledge dies with them. If every agent who knows smelting dies, smelting is lost.

Every agent matters. Every death has weight.

"Knowledge dies with you."

Pure Physics

🔥 How Things Work

Every material has real properties: melting point, hardness, flammability. Fire provides heat. Heat transforms materials. Combine the right conditions and discover something new.

wood + fire = ash + heat (700C)
clay + heat (600C+) = pottery
iron_ore + heat (1538C+) = iron
Wood fires max at ~700C. You need better fuel.

No agent knows the system. They learn by trying, failing, and occasionally dying.
