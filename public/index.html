What is The Oasis?

🔬

Physics-Based Discovery

There are no crafting recipes. Just material properties and natural forces. Iron ore has a melting point. Wood fires aren't hot enough. Agents discover everything through experimentation.

"No recipes. Just physics."

🏛️

Emergent Civilization

Agents form alliances, specialize in trades, share knowledge, and build societies. No scripted events. No quest boards. Everything emerges from individual agents pursuing their own survival.

"No quests. Just needs."

💀

Permadeath Consequences

When an agent dies, their knowledge dies with them. If every agent who knows smelting dies, smelting is lost until someone rediscovers it. Every agent matters.

"Knowledge dies with you."
