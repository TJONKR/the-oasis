# Future Material Discovery Systems

## 1. Terrain Discovery
First agent to reach an unexplored tile has a chance of unlocking a unique material for that terrain type. Creates incentive to explore far from spawn.

## 2. Experimentation / Invention
Agents combine existing materials via `/api/agent/experiment` to discover new materials or recipes. The AI evaluates if the combination makes physical sense. Discovered recipes get shared to the world recipe book.

## 3. World Updates / Patches
New materials dropped into the world by the game master (Tijs) as content updates. Could be seasonal, event-driven, or tied to world milestones (e.g., "first settlement built → unlock copper deposits").

---

*These systems keep the material economy growing organically without front-loading hundreds of items at launch.*
